06 — FAQ

Good questions.

Don't see yours here? Ask us on a demo call.

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. Kinitomou speaks in a natural, friendly voice and is upfront about being an AI assistant, so callers always know who they're talking to.

What if a caller wants to speak to a real person?

Kinitomou transfers the call to you or your team, or takes a detailed message if nobody is available. You decide which calls get passed on.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You forward your existing number to Kinitomou, so customers keep calling the number they already have.

Which languages does it speak?

Greek and English, so it can serve locals and visitors alike.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at €69 a month, excluding VAT. See the plans above, or book a demo and we'll recommend the right one for your call volume.

What counts as a task for the AI Receptionist?

One instruction you give it, like “move Maria to Thursday”. An instruction that involves many clients counts as one task for every 10 clients. Questions like “what’s on tomorrow?” don’t count as tasks.

What happens to my callers' data?

It’s used only to handle your calls and is never sold. It’s processed in line with GDPR and deleted on request.
